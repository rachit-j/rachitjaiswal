--- layout: project title: Scorpio project: scorpio permalink: /projects/scorpio/ --- ## Overview Scorpio is FIRST Tech Challenge work spanning the design, fabrication, and programming of a competition robot. ## Contribution As a captain and programming lead, Rachit worked on autonomous detection, computer vision, odometry-based movement, PID loops, and finite-state-machine control. Team results are intentionally kept separate from individual contribution; rankings and awards are not included here pending verification. ## Implementation The work uses OpenCV and FTC tooling to connect perception, sensor input, autonomous navigation, and field-tested robot behavior.
