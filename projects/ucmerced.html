--- layout: project title: AI Safety and Reliability Research at UC Merced project: ucmerced permalink: /projects/ucmerced/ --- ## Overview Research at UC Merced examined safety and reliability questions around large language models in safety-critical contexts. ## Contribution The existing public account describes evaluation of model behavior across edge cases and analysis of the risks of using model outputs in high-consequence settings. Precise scope, dates, and publishable outcomes are intentionally not expanded here. ## Reflection The work foregrounded precision, interpretability, and the importance of clearly bounded claims when evaluating intelligent systems.
