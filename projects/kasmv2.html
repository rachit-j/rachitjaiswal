KasmV2

Historical multi-school deploymentPoway Unified School District, May 2024–July 2025, Systems Engineer Intern

Built a multi-server AWS platform delivering desktops and applications through a browser across five high schools.

From a student’s browser to the cloud. Select a stage.

    The problem

    Students using low-powered Chromebooks need access to computing environments that can support programming and other coursework without requiring a powerful local machine.

    My contribution

    I initiated KasmV2 and engineered its cloud infrastructure. Docker and Nginx provided containerized environments and traffic routing. AWS Auto Scaling, load balancing, and Ansible/Terraform provisioning supported deployment and operation across multiple schools.

    Deployment and support

    The project began as a Del Norte High School pilot and expanded to five high schools in Poway Unified School District. It received support from North Island Credit Union and the PUSD Foundation.
